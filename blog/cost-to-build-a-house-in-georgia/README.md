# Cost to Build a House in Georgia — blog page

Built from the content brief "Cost to Build a House in Georgia" (HSD content sprint, September 2026), with the same layout and components as the other blog posts (shell copied from `blog/cost-to-build-a-house-in-arkansas/`). Lives in `blog/`, so links to the rest of the site start `../../`.
Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step and no dependencies.

Text follows the edited brief (tracked changes supplied as screenshots), not the original PDF text: e.g. "The cost to build a house in Georgia…", "project-specific", "builder-grade", US spelling (labor, specialized), and the reworded trade paragraphs.

## Sections

1. Hero: "Blog · Georgia cost guide" eyebrow, intro (second paragraph behind Read More), two buttons, and the navy house card (builder-grade $150–$270 and custom $270–$350+ per sq ft).
2. Trust logos and reviews (shared with the other pages).
3. Average cost: lead + 2 tiles ($300,000–$700,000; HomeGuide average $480,000).
4. How to estimate: 9 steps, the information-needed question with 8 cards, accordion (estimating without plans).
5. Estimating services: lead, the estimate-contents question with 8 cards, accordion (sitework and subcontractor costs).
6. Cost calculator: lead, accordion (final building cost).
7. Cost per square foot: lead, 3 tiles ($150–$270 builder-grade; $270–$350+ custom; 2,000 sq ft at $150 = $300,000), the factors question with 7 cards, accordion (land costs).
8. 3,000 sq ft homes: lead, 2 tiles ($450,000–$900,000; $150–$300 per sq ft), accordion (fixed cost).
9. Trade-by-trade costs: 12 cards (bullet summary shows; Read More reveals the numbered paragraph).
10. Cost factors: 4 tabs (main factors, materials and labor, lot and location, home design), each follow-up question in an accordion.
11. Additional costs: 7 cards, then the closing "What Determines the Final Cost" question.
